Run-on sentences are your biggest blocker

You join two full sentences with just a comma about 3 times per 100 words, and it is rising. Readers lose your idea in the middle. When you fix one in green pen it usually stays fixed, so the skill is there. The habit is not yet.

Same-shape sentences

Your sentences are long but they are all built the same way, joined with "and". Your variety score is in the bottom quarter of the class. Try: one short punch sentence per paragraph.

"I love collecting. Since I was little, I've collected all kinds of small items, like full sets of cards and tiny jewellery."

Your personal voice is real and consistent

Across all 11 pieces your I-driven, story-first register stays stable. That consistency is exactly what a personal statement needs. Protect it.

Almost no signposts

You use transition words about once per 100 words; the class median is three. Paragraphs feel like lists because nothing tells the reader how idea 2 relates to idea 1. Your next essay: aim for one however/therefore/for example per paragraph.
